Safety findings that changed the architecture

My friend says I love sushi. initially risked attribution poisoning → third-party guard.

Learned conflict classification once tried to supersede an unrelated preference with a goal → structural safety gates.

Same-predicate values such as I love sushi + I love jazz are now additive by default rather than incorrectly treated as drift.

All numeric results here are from synthetic, deterministic, or local stress evaluations included in this repository. No human-user performance claim is made.
